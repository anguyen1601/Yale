YALE HARKNESS TOWER
Harkness Tower was built between 1917 and 1921 by James
Gamble Rogers (Class of 1889). Modeled on St. Botolph's
tower in Boston, England, it was conceived as a memorial to
a recently deceased alumnus, Charles W. Harkness.
